Why I made it

I learned time blocking in a time management training and did it by hand every week, on paper. It worked. Then life got busy and I stopped. I built this to make the habit easier to come back to.

— Mahmoudou Sidibe

See it in one minute

How it works

  1. Block your week

    Click or drag on empty time to add a block. Give it a name and a category, like Deep work or Meetings.

  2. Adjust as you go

    Drag blocks to move them, pull the bottom edge to resize, and tick them off when they're done.

  3. Reuse good weeks

    Copy this week to next week, or save it as a template you can apply in one click.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. No cost, no account, no ads. It's open source under the MIT license.

Where is my plan saved?

Only in the browser you use it in, on your own device. Nothing is sent to me or anyone else. Use Back up in the planner now and then to save a copy as a file, and Restore to load it on another device.

Should I open it online or download it?

Opening it online is easiest and works on phones. Download the file if you want to use it offline on your computer. The two keep separate plans, so pick one and stick with it, or move between them with Back up and Restore.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Swipe to move around the week, tap a block to edit it, and press and hold a block to move it.

Can I share it with my clients, team or students?

Yes, please do. Share the link, or download the file and change it to suit your group. The MIT license allows that, as long as the license note stays in the code.
