I've quit planners before. Why would this one stick?

Because changing the plan is how you use it, not a sign you failed. Drag a block when the meeting moves. Nothing is ever marked missed or overdue, and there are no streaks to break. Step away for a month and your weeks are where you left them; start again from last week in one click.

I already use the free planner. Is Pro worth $45 a year?

If you plan on one computer and back up now and then, the free planner may be all you need, and it stays free. Pro earns its keep when you want the week on your phone too, never want to think about backup files, or want to see the week you planned next to the week you had. It works out at less than $4 a month.

Will it take an afternoon to set up?

No. It's the planner you already know. Sign in, bring your weeks with the free planner's Backup file, and you're back on the week you left. There's nothing to configure first.

Is there an app for my phone?

Pro runs in your phone's browser and opens on the Day view. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app, with nothing to install from an app store.

Does it sync with Google Calendar?

Not live. As in the free planner, you can send a week to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook as a calendar file.
